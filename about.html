Cultural Food

Dastarkhawn with Semi desi food

Dastarkhawn is a traditional dining setup in South Asian culture, particularly in Pakistan. It is a large, often ornate, cloth spread on the floor where meals are served and shared among family and friends. The Dastarkhawn holds cultural significance as it fosters a sense of community and togetherness during meals. It is a symbol of hospitality and is often used during special occasions and gatherings to create a warm and inviting atmosphere for sharing food and stories.
