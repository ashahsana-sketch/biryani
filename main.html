Welcome to our special Karachi Biryani recipe! This dish is a flavorful and aromatic blend of spices, tender chicken, and fragrant basmati rice. Perfect for any occasion, this biryani will transport your taste buds to the vibrant streets of Karachi. Follow our step-by-step guide to create this delicious meal in your own kitchen.

Fun Facts about Biryani:

  • Biryani is believed to have originated in the Indian subcontinent and has many regional variations.
  • The word "biryani" is derived from the Persian word "birian," which means "fried before cooking."
  • Karachi Biryani is known for its spicy and tangy flavor, often achieved by using yogurt and tomatoes.
  • In some regions, biryani is traditionally cooked in a sealed pot (Dum) to enhance the flavors.
  • Biryani is often served during special occasions and festivals in South Asia.

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 inch ginger, grated
  • 2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves and mint for garnish

Steps to make Biryani:

  1. Soak basmati rice in water for 30 minutes, then drain.
  2. Heat oil in a large pot and fry sliced onions until golden brown. Remove half for garnish.
  3. Add garlic and ginger paste to the pot and sauté for a minute.
  4. Add chicken, tomatoes, yogurt, and spices. Cook until chicken is partially done.
  5. Add fresh herbs and sprinkle fried onions back on top.
  6. Boil rice in a separate pot until 70% done, then drain.
  7. Layer rice over the chicken, cover tightly, and steam (Dum) on low heat for 20–25 minutes.
  8. Let it rest for 5 minutes, then fluff and serve.

Serving Tips:

Serve Biryani piping hot with chilled raita or a fresh kachumber salad.

Lahori Biryani

Lahori Tikka Biryani is a flavorful variation of the traditional biryani, originating from the city of Lahore in Pakistan. It is known for its rich and aromatic spices, tender meat (usually chicken or mutton), and fragrant basmati rice. The dish is often garnished with fried onions, fresh coriander, and mint leaves, adding to its vibrant flavor profile. Lahori Biryani is a beloved dish that showcases the culinary heritage of Lahore and is enjoyed by food enthusiasts around the world.

Tips:

  • Use long-grain basmati rice
  • Wash rice 3–4 times to remove excess starch
  • Soak for 20–30 minutes before cooking
  • Cook rice only 70–80% done before layering — overcooked rice becomes mushy
